Wataru Miyahara (宮原 渉)

Pharmacy student working on machine-learning prediction of drug adverse events and compound properties.

Faculty of Pharmacy, Meiji Pharmaceutical University (expected graduation: March 2028)
Member of the Department of Medical Molecular Informatics

Research Philosophy

  • Checking the basis of predictions: Besides predictive accuracy, I care about whether the basis of a model's prediction can be checked against chemical structures (interpretability), and I also test how far that basis holds up.
  • Current work: Building adverse-event prediction models with Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN) and fragment features, and examining the basis of their predictions.
