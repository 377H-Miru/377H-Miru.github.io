Wataru Miyahara (宮原 渉)

A researcher working on interpretable machine learning for drug discovery and toxicology.

Faculty of Pharmacy, Meiji Pharmaceutical University (expected graduation: March 2028)
Member of the Department of Medical Molecular Informatics

Research Philosophy

  • Machine learning that drives scientific discovery: Beyond pursuing predictive accuracy, I focus on interpretable machine learning that explains why a model makes a prediction in chemically and biologically meaningful terms. Rather than treating AI as a black box, I aim to use it as an exploration tool that helps researchers gain new insights, contributing to drug discovery and safety assessment.
  • Main approaches: Identification of toxicophores using the interpretability of Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN), optimization of structural descriptors, and multimodal analysis with graph neural networks such as GINE-Net.
